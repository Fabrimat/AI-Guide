This module is made of chapters for specific jobs. Each chapter starts from a task people in that job repeat every week, shows it from rough notes to the finished document, and walks through the steps on the tool that job really uses. The goal is that you redo it the following week on your own, without coming back to the guide.

The general lessons (writing a good prompt, privacy, drafts, tables) are not repeated: when they are needed, you get a link.

## Start here

[Your work assistant](/en/by-profession/your-work-assistant/) applies to any job. It explains how to create a Project or an assistant with your own instructions, which files to upload and how to update it. Then pick the chapter for your job.
