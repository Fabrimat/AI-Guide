<LessonSummary>

- Write Claude your client notes as you take them on the phone, without tidying them up: it puts them into the template.
- You always supply the prices. Claude does not know them, and if you ask, it invents them. Have it write [TO BE CONFIRMED] where data is missing.
- Ask for two variants (cheaper and more comfortable) and a list of what is missing before you send the quote.
- Always reread: dates, figures and names.

</LessonSummary>

This lesson assumes the project from [Set up Claude for your agency](/en/by-profession/travel-agency/start-here/), with your quote template already uploaded. If you have not done it, start there.

The case: a family calls, they want a week in Puglia in June. You jot notes quickly. Between the phone call and the written quote there is the job of putting your notes in order. Claude does that for you, as long as you tell it clearly what to do and what not to do.

## From notes to quote

Open a chat in the "Agenzia Rondine" project and paste the notes as you wrote them. These are for the Bianchi family, invented:

```text
Bianchi family, two adults and two kids (12 and 15).
Puglia, 7 nights, leaving Saturday 12 June 2027, back Saturday 19.
Own car from Bologna. Budget about 2,500 euro in total.
They want sea but also a couple of visits (Alberobello, Lecce).
They do not like big hotels. The younger boy is coeliac.
Half board only if it does not push the price up too much.
```

And right below it, the request:

```text
Write the quote in the agency template. You do not know prices: where data is missing write [TO BE CONFIRMED]. At the end give me a list of everything I still need to know or verify before sending it.
```

Claude answers with the quote split into the sections of your template (client, trip, accommodation, services included, price, terms) and, at the bottom, a list like this:

```text
To verify before sending:
1. Accommodation price: no price given, I need the rate for 4 people, 7 nights.
2. Availability for 12 June.
3. Whether the property offers gluten-free menus (the younger boy is coeliac).
4. Cost of any entrance or parking at Alberobello and Lecce.
5. The property's cancellation terms.
```

The list is the most useful part. Anyone can fill in a template, but the list reminds you of the coeliac question, which was in your notes and would have vanished from the quote.

## You supply the prices

You get prices from suppliers (tour operators, properties, carriers). When you have them, write them in the same chat: "Accommodation A: 1,480 euro for 7 nights with breakfast. Accommodation B: 1,790 euro with half board. Update the quote." Claude inserts them and redoes the totals. Check the sums by hand or with a calculator: on long sums it can get it wrong.

## Ask for variants

With the same data you can ask: "Give me two variants: a cheaper one and a more comfortable one, with the differences in three lines." The client chooses, you do not redo the work. If a variant does not convince you, say what to change ("B without half board, with one free dinner in Lecce") and Claude rewrites it.

## Bring it into Word

You have two ways.

**Copy and paste.** Select the quote text in the chat, copy it and paste it into a Word document with your template. It is the simplest and always works.

**A ready-made Word file.** If file creation is on in your account, you can write "Give it to me as a Word file" and download it from the conversation. The option is called "Code execution and file creation" and is in **Settings**, **Capabilities** section; it is usually on. Verified in November 2026, but labels change: if you cannot find it, use copy and paste. Either way, open the file and check the layout, because it does not always match your template.

<Collapsible title="What to write if Claude changes the template's sections">

Add to the project instructions: "In quotes keep exactly the titles and order of the sections of the uploaded template. Do not add sections." Then try again with the same notes. If it keeps happening, the uploaded template is probably unclear: try a version in which the sections have sharp titles.

</Collapsible>

## Before you send it

Always do this check: first and last name spelled correctly, dates and weekdays consistent, number of people, every figure compared with the supplier, cancellation terms that match yours. Then repeat for every quote, even if the previous ones were right.

For the client use only the data the quote needs. The full rule is in [Set up Claude for your agency](/en/by-profession/travel-agency/start-here/#the-rule-on-client-data).
