<LessonSummary>

- Gemini does not remember past chats: you save the shop's rules once, in a skill or in a text to paste at the start of a chat.
- The instructions say what you sell, how you talk to customers and what never to promise (delivery dates, product properties).

You run a shop selling local products and gifts, with a small online shop and an Instagram page. You write product descriptions, answer customers, chase suppliers and check what is running out. The products change, while your way of talking and your rules stay. It pays to write them once and have Gemini read them in every chat.

The general picture is in [Your work assistant](/en/by-profession/your-work-assistant/). Here we do it with Gemini, for a shop.

Throughout the series we use an invented shop, **Bottega Fiordaliso**, run by Paolo: local food products and gift ideas. Customers, suppliers and orders are made up. The shop is Italian, so the examples use Italian products and customs.

## Save the instructions as a skill

Google is replacing Gems with **skills**. If you already have a Gem: from 17 November 2026, on personal Google accounts, Gems turn into skills and become read-only (you can open them, but not use or edit them). On 17 December 2026 they are removed from the Gemini apps. For work accounts (Workspace) the dates are later. So do not build anything new on a Gem.

To create a skill, if your account offers it:

1. In Gemini's left-hand menu click **Personalizza** (Customize), then **Skill**.
2. Click **Crea** (Create), then **Crea manualmente** (Create manually).
3. Give it a name (for example "Bottega Fiordaliso"), paste the instructions below and click **Crea**.

Verified in November 2026 on the Italian interface; the English names may differ slightly. Google changes the names and positions of these entries often: if they differ, look in the customization menu for the ones about skills. With a free account it is not certain that creating one is available, and for under-18s the feature arrives later.

## If you do not have skills

The method that never breaks: a text of instructions in a Google Doc, which you copy and paste as the first message of every new chat. It takes ten seconds and works with any version of Gemini.

Gemini also has memory: click your photo, then **Contesto personale** (Personal context), and turn on the switch under **Memoria** (Memory). It needs an adult personal account with activity saving on. It is meant for things about you, and it can keep what you write: if you use it, put no customer data in the chats. A wider picture is in [Privacy basics](/en/fundamentals/privacy-basics/).

## Instructions for a shop

A template to adapt. Short sentences, as if you were telling a new shop assistant.

<Collapsible title="Ready-made instructions for Bottega Fiordaliso">

```text
You work with me, Paolo, owner of Bottega Fiordaliso, a small shop selling local food products and gift ideas, with an online shop and an Instagram page. We write in Italian.

What you do: you help me write product descriptions, Instagram posts, replies to customers and emails to suppliers, and keep the stock sheet in order.

Product categories: honey and preserves, pasta and flours, sweets, drinks, gift boxes.

Tone with customers: friendly and direct, formal "lei", short sentences, like a small-town shopkeeper who knows his products. No slogans, no "unique experience", no strings of exclamation marks.

Products: use only the information I give you. Never invent ingredients, origin, certifications (organic, DOP and the like), awards or health effects. If a fact is missing write [TO BE CONFIRMED] and at the end of the answer list what is missing.

Deliveries and prices: never promise a delivery date, a refund or a discount that I did not write myself. You do not know the prices: do not invent figures.

Customer data: use only the first name and what the reply needs. If I paste an address, phone number or email by mistake, warn me and do not use it.

If information is missing, ask me before writing.
```

</Collapsible>

## What not to paste

The texts you write end up in the chats. For a shop there are two typical cases. Customer data: shipping address, phone, email, names tied to an order. And suppliers' confidential prices, if you prefer to keep them in your own sheet: in emails to a supplier you can write [AGREED PRICE] and fill it in yourself.

For doubtful cases read [Privacy and company data](/en/at-work/company-data-and-privacy/). If you have a text full of data to clean up, [the anonymizer](/en/at-work/company-data-and-privacy/#how-to-anonymize-in-practice) runs in your browser.

## Try it now

Open a new chat (with the skill active, or paste the instructions text) and write: "Introduce yourself in three lines and tell me what you understood about my shop and what you are missing". If Gemini does not mention the tone or the categories, the instructions are too vague: add a sentence and try again.

If after a few weeks you notice it always ends posts with an over-enthusiastic line, add one line: "No enthusiastic closing lines". It applies to all future chats.
