Guests ask the same questions every week: what is the Wi-Fi, how does the heating work, when is the rubbish collected. Giulia has all of it scattered across phone notes and old messages. A welcome guide of a page or two saves her half of her messages. ChatGPT can put it in order, but the facts stay hers.

If you already have the [ChatGPT instructions for your B&B](/en/by-profession/holiday-rental/start-here/), keep them active. Otherwise paste your house facts at the start of the chat.

## The starting notes

These are Giulia's notes, as she typed them on her phone, messy on purpose:

```text
wifi: name Glicine_ospiti, password I write it
heating: knob in the corridor, guests do not touch the boiler
rubbish: wet waste, plastic, paper, glass, days to check on the council website
parking: courtyard, two spaces, if full via del Prato
pharmacy: in the square, hours to check
quiet after 10 pm
check-out by 10, keys in the box
emergencies: my number [PHONE]
```

Notice the parts where Giulia did not write a fact: rubbish days, pharmacy hours. They are data that changes, and the AI cannot know them.

## The fixed template

The guide always has the same sections, in the same order. That way guests find what they need at once, and when something changes you know where to go.

<Collapsible title="The guide template, to paste into ChatGPT">

```text
Reorder my notes into a welcome guide for guests, with these sections, in this order:

1. Welcome (two friendly lines)
2. Arrival and departure (times, keys)
3. Wi-Fi
4. Heating and air conditioning
5. Rubbish (what to sort, days)
6. Parking
7. Pharmacy and nearby services
8. House rules (quiet hours, smoking, outside visitors)
9. If there is a problem (who to write to or call)

Rules:
- Use only the facts I give you. If a section has no data, write [TO BE CONFIRMED] and do not invent.
- Leave passwords and codes as [WIFI PASSWORD], [KEY BOX CODE]: I write them myself.
- Short sentences, friendly tone, informal address.
- At the end list what is missing.
```

</Collapsible>

Paste the template and the notes in the same chat. ChatGPT returns a text with the nine sections. Where data is missing you see `[TO BE CONFIRMED]` and, at the bottom, the list of what you must check: rubbish days on the council website, pharmacy hours from the pharmacy.

## From text to document

Copy the text into Word or Google Docs and do three things by hand:

1. Fill the square brackets with real data: password, code, phone, rubbish days.
2. Put the date of the last check at the top, for example "Updated June 2027".
3. Check the layout: real headings, a little space. Then print a copy for each room or share the document link with guests, as you prefer.

Information on obligations and taxes is not written by the AI. The amount of the tourist tax is decided by your own Comune (the local council): look at the council's website and write it yourself if you want it in the guide.

## Keeping it up to date

An old guide is worse than no guide. A few simple habits:

- **One file.** A copy in Word and one in Docs are two guides that contradict each other. Pick one.
- **A reminder at every change of season** to reread heating, rubbish, opening hours.
- **When something changes,** fix the document, and tell ChatGPT in a new chat with just the new lines if you want the welcome message to use them.
- **After a repeated question** from a guest ("where is the hair dryer?") add the line to the guide.

Now and then paste the guide into ChatGPT with the request: "What might be unclear to a foreign guest reading this for the first time?". It points out ambiguous sentences, and you decide on the fixes.
